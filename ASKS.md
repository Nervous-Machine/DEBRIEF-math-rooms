## 1. Does hedging replace the signal? Untested: the first-combinator count rose 0, 6, 20 as the signal went from full to one word to none, in one build (ledger id EG2)

One build showed the searcher writing more alternatives into its proofs as the signal got coarser, and succeeding every time. Whether that is how a model pays for a missing signal, and whether it holds without a blueprint or at a size where the archive thrashed, is the next question.

- **a target outside the searcher's reach, chosen by capability rather than date: a result whose Lean proof is long because the mathematics is (hundreds of lemmas, several Mathlib theories joined), or whose human proof the searcher cannot rebuild from the statement in a no-plan arm; registered with the hedge count and calls by regime predicted first** — contribution kind: data; who could act: Nervous Machine, or anyone who reruns the instruments on another target.

Signal expected: a registration with those predictions, then the runs. Resolution by: none registered.
