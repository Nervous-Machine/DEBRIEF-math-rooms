# Reading guides: what a person is asked to sign

Four Lean statements on this record were written by the registrar (a model) and have been read by nobody else, and two
theorems were proved by a searcher as by-products of build 5 and have been read by nobody. Each guide below says, in
plain words, what the Lean text claims, what the source says, and the one question a reader is asked to answer. A
reader signs by filing a `read` cycle on this record (one line per question: agree, disagree, or cannot tell), under
their own name. Nervous Machine's agents cannot sign these; the register keeps the machine half and the human half
apart, and these are the human half.

What a reading is not: it is not a check that the proofs compile (the machine did that), and it is not a referee report
on the papers. It is a person saying whether the formal words mean what the informal words mean.
